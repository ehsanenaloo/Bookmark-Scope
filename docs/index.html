Chrome bookmark extension · User guide

Know what you’ve saved.
Keep it tidy.

Bookmark Scope shows every bookmark you already have for the site you are on, and gives you a full dashboard to find duplicates, check for dead links, tag, back up and restore, all on your own device.

Start in ten minutes →Browse all guides

Free and open source (MIT). No account, no server, no analytics.

What you can do

  • See bookmarks for this page, host or domain in one click
  • Merge duplicates with a preview and a survivor you choose
  • Find untitled, old and look-alike bookmarks
  • Check links only after you grant permission, with a resumable scan
  • Take a snapshot of your folders and tags and restore it later
  • Use it in 52 languages, including right-to-left
